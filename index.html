ABOUT THE FIRM

Rooted in integrity. Driven by practical commercial judgment.

Derived from the Latin Amicus ("friend"), our philosophy centers on steadfast, candid counsel. In modern corporate environments, legal advice cannot exist in an isolated silo; it must synchronize with regulatory realities, corporate valuations, and strategic operational objectives.

Founded and led by Vikas Gupta, Advocate, the firm brings together multidisciplinary perspective across commercial transactions, Companies Act mandates, securities regulations, and high-stakes dispute representation across India’s benches and tribunals.
